How to play

Crown the board so every rank, file and coloured realm holds exactly one Queen.

I

One Queen per rank, file & realm

Each row, each column, and each colour region gets exactly one Queen — no more, no fewer.

II

Queens can’t touch

No two Queens may sit in adjacent squares — not horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

III

Controls

Click / tap cycles a square: empty → × → Queen → empty. Double-click or long-press drops a Queen at once. Right-click toggles an ×. Click & drag to sweep × marks across squares.

IV

Helpers

Rank & file numbers strike through when solved. Rule-breaking Queens flash red. Undo / redo, a move counter and a timer track your run.

Smart Auto-X auto-marks a faint × around every Queen you place — the 8 touching squares (Adjacent), or the whole rank, file & realm too (Aggressive). Your own × marks are never touched, and removing a Queen clears only its auto-marks — ones another Queen still rules out, or that you’ve tapped to keep, stay put.

V

Royal Decrees mode

Switch on Royal Decrees and each new round a herald proclaims one rule-bending decree:

The Fog of War

Realm colours stay hidden until you touch a square inside them.

The Royal Guard

One fixed Guard holds its square — it blocks Queens like a permanent ×.

Assassin’s Range

Queens also strike a square two steps away along any diagonal.

Color Lock

Placing a Queen auto-marks × on the rest of its realm.

Turn on Speed decrees to have the decree change every 30 seconds mid-round.
